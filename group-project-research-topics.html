Group Project Research Topics

Construct sentiment from conference call transcripts. Does positive sentiment predict higher stock prices?

Difficulty: 3☆

Pros:

  • hot topic in empirical asset pricing and accounting, lots of good papers to refer to

  • source of the text is listed companies, high quality and easy to clean

Do firms that have mostly good Glassdoor reviews perform better than firms with bad reviews?

Difficulty: 4☆

Pros:

  • interesting topic, useful for future job seeking
  • textual dataset is available (Kaggle，GitHub)

Cons:

  • moderate to upper level, slightly challenging

Let LLM to extract summaries from long texts such as annual reports and research reports

Difficulty: 3☆

Pros:

  • interesting topic, useful for future work
  • source of the text is listed companies, high quality and easy to clean (SEC 10-K)

Cons:

  • need to deal with problems such as Attention Weight Decay

Prof comment:

  • Donot use prompt engineering, download some pre-trained models from Hugging Face, then do some fine-tune or other jobs by ourselves.

Forecast future stock price using large models to based on financial news

Difficulty: 5☆

Pros:

  • hot topic in empirical asset pricing and accounting, lots of good papers to refer to
  • interesting topic, useful for future work

Cons:

  • difficulty in obtaining free dataset (financial news with good labels for listed companies)
  • find free LLM API, or resources needed to locally deploy open source models

Prof comment:

  • Good idea. Donot use ChatGPT, locally deploy some open source model such as DeepSeek. You can try it.
